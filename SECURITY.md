# Security Policy

## 🛡️ Research Disclaimer
This repository contains research into Bring Your Own Vulnerable Driver (BYOVD) techniques. It is intended solely for educational purposes, defensive engineering, and authorized security auditing.

## Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a security issue within the research tools provided here, please open an Issue.

**Note on CVEs:** This repository documents vulnerabilities found in third-party drivers (Safetica, TechPowerUp). We are not the maintainers of those drivers. For vulnerabilities regarding those specific products, please contact the respective vendors or the CERT Coordination Center.

## Policy for Use
- Do NOT use this research for unauthorized activities.
- Do NOT distribute the vulnerable binaries associated with these hashes.
- Always perform research in an isolated, non-production environment.
